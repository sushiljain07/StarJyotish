# Star Jyotish UI audit: layout, mobile and colour (14 Nov 2026)

Audited `feat/phase5a-contrast-tokens` (PR #167) in Chromium (Playwright) at 1440×900, 1280×800, 1024×768, 768×1024, 390×844 and 360×740. English was checked at every width, Hindi at 1280 and 360.

Coverage:
- 192 public runs.
- 48 signed-in runs, using a simulated OTP login against the local dev DB (`OTP_DEV_MODE=true`).
- 6 runs with geolocation granted.
- Separate passes for interaction, empty and error states.

Raw data, scripts and screenshots were kept in the session scratchpad. They are not committed.

**Good news:** across all 246 runs there was no horizontal page overflow, no clipped text that hid meaning, and no page errors. The remaining problems are:
- density;
- legibility;
- colour that leaks around the 5a token system;
- a few empty or broken states.

Severity: **P0** means broken for everyone. **P1** clearly hurts UX. **P2** is polish. No P0 issues were found.

## 1. Top 10 by user impact

1. **L1 (P1): Signed-in mobile /kundli spends 42% of the screen on chrome.**
   - Header 61 + sticky band 131 + section bar 61 + app bottom nav 60 = 313 of 740 px.
   - After scroll-collapse it is still 33%. Guests lose 25–34%.
2. **S1 (P1): Pages go blank without a location.** /week-ahead is blank and the /home "This week" heading is empty when no location is set. Nothing prompts the user to set one.
3. **T1 (P1): Chart labels are unreadable on phones.**
   - The 500-unit SVG renders at 296 px, so planets are about 9 px, degrees about 6.5 px and transit labels about 5 px.
   - Sarvatobhadra uses 7–9 px. Devanagari is worse.
4. **C1 (P1): Inline `color:'#D9A441'` bypasses the 5a tokens.** It measures 2.0–2.2:1 on parchment (19 uses).
5. **C2 (P1): Planet colours are Tailwind defaults.**
   - Mercury and Venus measure 2.8–3.1:1; Sun and Moon 3.5–4.1:1.
   - The two colour maps disagree: Moon is red in one and purple in the other; Venus is green in one and pink in the other.
6. **C3 (P1): /home fails contrast badly.**
   - 55–58 contrast failures per load. Alpha text on night goes as low as 1.77:1.
   - Sage, mauve and vermillion are used on night at 2.8–3.9:1.
   - 74–88 text nodes are under 12 px, including 7.5 and 8 px.
7. **A1 (P1): A transient refresh failure signs the user out.** A 429, 5xx or network error on `/api/auth/refresh` redirects to /login. I reproduced it (AUTH_LIMIT is 20/min per IP).
8. **L2 (P1): Floating buttons cover content.** The "Ask Jyoti" pill (54 px) and scroll-to-top sit above the bottom nav and cover card text and the "Ask →" / "Read →" links.
9. **L3 (P1): At 768 px the signed-in header wraps** "My Charts" and "AI Insights" onto two lines.
10. **T2 (P1): Many mobile tap targets are under 44 px.** Examples:
    - sub-tab pills 30 px;
    - Refresh 28 px;
    - Panchang pills 32 px;
    - footer accordions 16 px;
    - Account "Edit" 21×16.

## 3. Colour scheme

**Coherent:**
- The night indigo, saffron gold, parchment and warm ink palette fits the product.
- The marketing pages read as one product.
- 5a works wherever it was applied (Panchang, Account, Pricing, Learn).
- Gold CTAs with night text measure about 8:1.

**Drift:**
1. Hard-coded colour the token tests can't see:
   - 19 inline `#D9A441`;
   - 10 /home components with embedded `<style>` hex/rgba;
   - an inline NavBar rgba;
   - zebra rows `bg-[#F8F6F2]` and `bg-[#F1ECE1]`;
   - `bg-slate-400/40`.
2. Foreign palettes for planets and sky timings: planetColors.js, planetsHubContent.js and QuickPanchangStrip, plus the Sarvatobhadra markers.
3. Section accents compete. Birth Chart uses gold, Analysis sage and Insights mauve, and Insights → Reports puts gold pills inside a mauve tab.
4. Three golds are in use (#D9A441, #BD8A2E as a resting background, #F0CB80).
5. There are no night text tokens, so everything secondary on night uses alpha /20–/60.
6. Radius and shadow tokens are bypassed:
   - `rounded-card` is used 10 times, against `rounded-2xl` 69 and `rounded-xl` 44;
   - shadow tokens are used 9 times, against more than 100 raw shadows.
7. Type drift:
   - sans bold headings on Insights and Rajyogas;
   - `font-mono` in Planet degrees and the /home masthead;
   - `text-[10px]` ×29, `text-[11px]` ×70, `text-[9px]` ×4, `text-[8px]` ×1.

**Token recommendations:**
- Night text:
  - `ink.onnightMuted` #A39CB4 (6.4:1 on night);
  - `ink.onnightFaint` about #8C86A0, for decoration.
- Night accents:
  - `good.onnight` #9FC7A2;
  - `bad.onnight` #E09090;
  - `mauve.onnight` #D4A5BC.
- One planet group, AA on parchment, used by both maps:

  | Planet | Colour | Contrast |
  |---|---|---|
  | Sun | #B83232 | 5.8:1 |
  | Moon | #6B4BA8 | 6.5:1 |
  | Mars | #8B2A2A | 8.4:1 |
  | Mercury | good #3F6B45 | |
  | Jupiter | primary-ink #86621E | |
  | Venus | mauve #8C5B73 | |
  | Saturn | #2F5AA8 | 6.6:1 |
  | Rahu | #4A4458 | |
  | Ketu | #7A4A2A | |

  Add lighter night variants for /home.
- `parchment.alt` #F1ECE1 for zebra rows.
- `primary.dark` for hover only.
- Codemods: `rounded-2xl` → `rounded-card`; `shadow-sm` → `card`; `shadow-md/lg` → `lift`.
- Selection is always gold. Sage and mauve stay as content tints.
- Tests should fail on JSX `color:'#…'`, on `<style>` hex, and on meaningful text of 8–10 px.

## 4. Layout

**Laptop (1440, 1280, 1024):**
- App pages use max-w-5xl well.
- The grid is inconsistent: the header is 6xl on marketing pages and 5xl in the app, Landing sections vary from 510 to 1120 px, and Panchang, About, Contact and FAQ start about 160 px right of the logo. Use one outer container per page and `max-w-prose` only for long text.
- /home is a 600 px column at 1280–1440, and its charts carousel is cut off with no arrows. Use two columns from `lg`.
- Short pages leave a night band under the footer. Make `main` `flex-1`.
- /insights has no way back to the chart.
- Headers and footers are mixed inconsistently.

**Mobile (360, 390) and tablet (768):**
- No overflow, and tables scroll internally.
- The main problems, in order: L1 chrome density, L2 floating buttons, T2 tap targets, T1 chart legibility.
- The sticky first columns work. L4 covers the Planets table.
- Tablet: the signed-in header wraps (L3).
- Hindi holds up apart from T1, L5, X7 and X2.
- Empty states: Panchang without a location is friendly and 404 is clear. Week-ahead without a location is broken (S1). The AI error is graceful but entirely vermillion.

## 5. Not checked

- Blog article: no articles in the local DB.
- AI content: LLM keys are broken locally, so only failure rendering was judged.
- The Sarvatobhadra transit column: the audit load produced 429s.
- MobileTimelineCard: never rendered, because its only importer, QuickPanchangStrip, is itself never imported.
- Google sign-in: no client_id locally.
- Real devices: iOS safe areas, zoom and font scaling were emulated only.
- The PDF output.
- Admin and astrologer dashboards.
